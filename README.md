**Main features**

- `plotext` draws its plots **inside the terminal**, as colored text, and its [installation](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/install.html) needs **nothing beside itself**; only pictures and videos ask for the [optional extras](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/install.html#extras).
- **Basic plots**: [scatter](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/basic.html#scatter), [line](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/basic.html#line) and [stem](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/basic.html#stem), each returning a [signal](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/signal.html) you configure and then draw.
- **Bars**: [simple](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bar.html#simple-bar), [labeled](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bar.html#labeled-bars), [floating](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bar.html#floating-bars), [multiple](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bar.html#multiple-bar) and [stacked](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bar.html#stacked-bar), with [histograms](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bar.html#histogram) and [box plots](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bar.html#box).
- **Specialized plots**: [error bars](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/specialized.html#error), [event plots](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/specialized.html#event), [heatmaps](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/specialized.html#heatmap), [confusion matrices](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/specialized.html#confusion-matrix) and [indicators](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/specialized.html#indicator).
- **Shapes and text**: [rectangles](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/shape.html#rectangle), [polygons](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/shape.html#polygon), [segments](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/shape.html#segment) and [lines](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/shape.html#shape-line) in five [line styles](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/shape.html#line-styles), plus text written anywhere on the plot.
- **Dates**: a [date and time axis](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/date.html), and the [candlestick](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/date.html#candlestick) chart of financial prices.
- **Media**: [pictures](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/media.html#image), [gifs](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/media.html#gif) and [video](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/media.html#video) with its sound, YouTube included.
- **Live plots**: [streaming](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/stream.html) that redraws in place, and [text animated](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/colorize.html#effects) by a moving effect.
- **Plot settings**: the [size](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/size.html), the [title and labels](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/label.html), the [rulers](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/ruler.html) and their [ticks](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/ruler.html#ticks), the [axes](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/axis.html), the [canvas](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/canvas.html) and its [legend](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/canvas.html#legend), twelve [themes](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/theme.html) and [your own](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/theme.html#custom-themes).
- **Subplots**: a [grid of plots](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/subplot.html#subplots), each panel holding a grid of its own, to any number of levels, and each with its own [size](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/size.html), [theme](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/theme.html) and settings.
- **Higher resolution**: [markers](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/marker.html#resolutions) splitting each character into a grid of sub-points, fitting four to eight times the data in the same space.
- **Colored primitives**: the [pixel](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/pixel.html#pixel) holding a color pair and a style, [colored strings](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/colorize.html#colorize), the [matrix](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/matrix.html#matrix) of colored characters a plot is made of, and the [marker](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/marker.html#markers) drawn at each point.
- **Terminal**: read its [size](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/terminal.html#terminal-size), [clear](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/terminal.html#terminal-clearing) it, and catch a [key press](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/terminal.html#is-pressed) without waiting.
- **Tools**: [test data](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/simulate.html), a [file toolkit](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/file.html), a [command line tool](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/cli.html) drawing a plot with no Python written, [pretty docstrings](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/prettydoc.html) browsable in a menu, and recipes for [other packages](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/packages.html).

Here is the link of the main documentation: **[plotext.readthedocs.io](https://plotext.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)**.
